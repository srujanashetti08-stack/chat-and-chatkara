Our Story

Built by Srujana, one plate at a time

Chat & Chatkara began as a simple idea: give KLE College students and the Chikodi neighbourhood a place for honest, tasty street food without waiting in a restaurant queue or paying restaurant prices.

Every golgappa, every Maggi bowl, every pazzta plate is made fresh at the cart — no pre-cooked batches, no shortcuts. Just Srujana, her spices, and a menu built around what students actually crave between classes.

Mission

Serve fresh, affordable chaat & snacks to every student and family nearby.

Vision

Become Chikodi's most-loved street food name, one satisfied customer at a time.
